From the finish to your next start.

Six stops inside the app. These screenshots use authored example recordings, notes and route data — not a real drive. No satellite or game-installation images.

  1. 01

    Choose your rally folder

    Point Rally Coach at your own RBR / RSF installation. Inspect the folder, then confirm it. The coach reads your files without changing the game.

  2. 02

    Find your last runs

    Start with your local history, or choose an activity: review a run, prepare a stage, learn a technique or explore a setup.

  3. 03

    Talk through a stage

    Choose a recorded section. Read measured observations first, then the interpretation and its limits. A pedal overlap alone is not proof of a mistake.

  4. 04

    Look at the evidence

    Inspect speed and pedal traces over a shared distance window. Route and driven-line views depend on matching data; missing channels or uncertain alignment stay explicit. This example has imagery switched off.

  5. 05

    Read your notes before the start

    Choose installed pacenotes and a reading notation. Review the calls and warnings before driving. Notes describe the road; they are not a proven speed or braking target.

  6. 06

    Take one exercise with you

    Explore a technique and a concrete practice instruction. Debrief exercises can be kept in a local plan. Planned does not mean driven, and improvement is not assumed.

Unsigned alpha — check before opening

Download SHA256SUMS and verify your installer. Only bypass a warning for a build you trust. These alpha builds are unsigned; macOS builds are also unnotarized. Organization policy may prevent opening them.

  • Windows: SmartScreen may show “Windows protected your PC”. For a trusted build, choose More info → Run anyway, if available.
  • macOS: drag the app from the DMG to Applications. Right-click → Open, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  • Linux: make the AppImage executable. Without FUSE, use --appimage-extract and run squashfs-root/AppRun. The .deb needs compatible WebKitGTK libraries.

Your recordings stay with you.

Import, analysis, coaching and history run locally. No telemetry uploads, account, analytics, remote crash reports or automatic updates. Maps are the only online data source: cached maps work offline; missing map data is explained and narrows dependent advice. The local core does not require map coverage.

This website has no analytics, cookies or external fonts. Downloads and issue reports open GitHub.
