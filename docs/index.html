Six stops.

Authored example data, not a real drive. No satellite images and no images from the game.

  1. Carry on

    Your last run, recent runs and cars at a glance. One search for everything.

  2. Rallies

    Every pass in the order you drove it, with best times and recordings.

  3. Stage and map

    Length, surface and your times. Step through the notes; map and list stay together.

  4. Telemetry

    Speed and pedals stacked on one distance axis, also against a second run.

  5. Setups

    One setup at a glance, compare a second, and for each value why you would change it.

  6. Cars

    Your cars, the facts from your install and the setups that belong to them.

Unsigned alpha — check before opening

Download SHA256SUMS and verify your installer. Only bypass a warning for a build you trust. These alpha builds are unsigned; macOS builds are also unnotarized. Organization policy may prevent opening them.

  • Windows: SmartScreen may show “Windows protected your PC”. For a trusted build, choose More info → Run anyway, if available.
  • macOS: drag the app from the DMG to Applications. Right-click → Open, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  • Linux: make the AppImage executable. Without FUSE, use --appimage-extract and run squashfs-root/AppRun. The .deb needs compatible WebKitGTK libraries.

Your data stays with you.

Everything runs locally. No account, no uploads, no analytics, no automatic updates. Only maps come from the internet; cached maps also work offline.

This website has no analytics, cookies or external fonts. Downloads and issue reports open GitHub.
