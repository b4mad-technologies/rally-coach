Thank you.

The people and projects behind the installed data Rally Coach reads.

Rally Coach reads what the Richard Burns Rally and RallySimFans community built over two decades. Nothing here would exist without these people. Names are written as they appear in the credits shipped with RallySimFans.

Thank you to the RallySimFans team, admins and testers crew, and to everyone in the RSF community who builds stages, cars, sounds, plugins and pacenotes on their free time and helps others on the RSF Discord.
